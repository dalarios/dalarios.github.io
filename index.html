RESEARCH

From proteins
to movement.

ActiveDROPS

I’ve been working on ActiveDROPS, a project studying how motor proteins organize networks of filaments inside small droplets. We make the proteins from DNA, observe the motion they generate, and study how changes to protein architecture affect that behavior.
